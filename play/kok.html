The Kok clock

Explore how Photosystem II’s four-step cycle produces oscillations in oxygen yield. Adjust misses, double hits, and starting populations to see how the response changes.

Set misses and double hits to zero, then set the initial S₁ population to 100%. Oxygen yield peaks on flashes 3, 7, and 11 without damping. Increasing the miss probability spreads the population across the four states and reduces the oscillation.

Photosystem II measurements can combine a period-four donor signal with a period-two acceptor signal. Fitting their sum can bias estimates of reaction kinetics. In Ferrari, Russell & Vinyard (2026), we used the Empirical Wavelet Transform to separate these components before fitting. Select Separated components to view the signals used here.

This demonstration uses a Kok model with misses and double hits but no backward transitions. The period-two component is a schematic damped oscillation, not a full acceptor-side simulation or a tool for fitting experimental data.
