Conway’s rules

Each generation updates all cells at once, using their eight nearest neighbors:

  1. Fewer than two live neighbors: a live cell dies. (underpopulation)
  2. Two or three live neighbors: a live cell survives.
  3. More than three live neighbors: a live cell dies. (overpopulation)
  4. Exactly three live neighbors: a dead cell becomes alive. (reproduction)

B3/S23 means birth with three neighbors and survival with two or three. Use the rule editor to change those counts and explore other cellular automata.

Cell color indicates age. The Gosper gun emits a glider every 30 generations. With edge wrapping enabled, returning gliders can eventually collide with the gun.
