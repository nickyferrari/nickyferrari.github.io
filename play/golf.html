Life Golf

Five holes, one physics: pure Conway's Game of Life, running on the exact same engine as the lab. Place seed cells in the blue launch zone, press Launch, and satisfy the hole's goal before the generation clock runs out. Your score is the number of cells you spend. Par assumes a clean spaceship. Every hole is provably solvable. The test suite brute-forces a winning line before I am allowed to publish it.
