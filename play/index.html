The Playground

Two of these are my research, made poke-able. The rest are things I find beautiful for their own sake. Everything runs in your browser — no installs, nothing collected, just the models doing what they do.

From my research

Designing a light environment

Same mean, same photon dose, same Total Variation — one predictable, one not. The experimental design at the center of my dissertation, with the sliders exposed.

Open the designer →

The Kok clock

Photosystem II counts to four. Fire flashes at dark-adapted cells and oxygen comes out in a damped period-four oscillation — with a period-two signal hiding underneath it.

Run the flash train →

For the fun of it

Penrose tiling explorer

Two rhombi, five-fold symmetry, and a pattern that never repeats — generated live by subdividing Robinson triangles. Sliders for depth, zoom, rotation, and color.

Open the explorer →

Game of Life lab

Conway's classic plus its stranger cousins — HighLife, Seeds, Day & Night, or your own custom rule. Draw, drop in famous patterns, and watch.

Open the lab →

Life Golf

A puzzle game running on the same Life engine: seed a few cells, press launch, and get a signal to the green — in as few cells as possible.

Play a round →

All of these are plain JavaScript on a <canvas> — view source to see how they work. The two research demos share a model file that the site's test suite checks on every build: the Kok sequence has to peak on flash three, the populations have to stay normalized, and the Total Variation matching has to land within two percent. Fun is not an excuse for unverified math.
