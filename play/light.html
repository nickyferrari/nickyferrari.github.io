The matching rule

TV = Σ |I(t+1) − I(t)| E[TV] = (n−1) · 2σ/√π k transitions = TV / 2A

Total variation (TV) sums the absolute changes in intensity. The periodic sequence targets the stochastic sequence’s TV. The match is approximate because transitions occur at discrete time steps.
