Designing a light environment

Algae in a turbulent water column and leaves under a moving canopy never see constant light. But "fluctuating light" is not one treatment. A regular flicker and an unpredictable one can deliver identical average irradiance and total photon dose while being completely different environments to acclimate to. To ask whether unpredictability itself carries a cost, you first have to control everything else. This is the design I use to do that.
