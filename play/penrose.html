Penrose tiling explorer

Penrose tilings cover the plane with just two rhombus shapes, exhibit perfect five-fold symmetry, and never repeat. That is the discovery (Penrose, 1974) that order does not require periodicity. This one is generated live by recursively subdividing Robinson triangles. Each slider notch of depth multiplies the tile count by roughly the golden ratio squared.

The two rhombus shapes (thin 36°, thick 72°) appear in a ratio that tends to the golden ratio φ ≈ 1.618. That is one of many ways φ is woven through this pattern. Wikipedia has a good rabbit hole on Penrose tilings.
