Research experience

2022 – 2026
Graduate Research Assistant — Vinyard Lab, LSU Biological Sciences
  • Developed Python and MATLAB pipelines using the Empirical Wavelet Transform to separate overlapping PSII signals. Measured reaction kinetics with pump-probe spectroscopy, P700 absorption, and fast fluorescence transients.
  • Built a microcontroller-driven LED flash system with custom circuitry for sub-millisecond saturating pulses. Programmed eight-culture multicultivators to deliver periodic and stochastic light matched for total variation.
  • Designed and 3D-printed optical mounts, cuvette holders, and detector housings using parametric CAD.
  • Characterized C. reinhardtii mutants under dynamic light and developed acclimation protocols for fluorescence measurements.
  • Screened 3,618 photosynthetic bacterial genomes for PSII assembly factors using Python, Biopython, PostgreSQL, and high-performance computing.
  • Developed a machine-learning pipeline to identify and quantify starch granules in microscopy images.
2020 – 2022
Graduate Research Assistant — South Lab, LSU Biological Sciences
  • Measured photosynthesis in Arabidopsis thaliana photorespiratory mutants under drought, heat, and fluctuating light using fluorescence imaging and gas exchange. Completed LI-COR training in gas exchange theory and instrument models.
  • Measured expression of photorespiratory bypass genes under stress using qPCR and cDNA libraries.
  • Analyzed genotype–environment interactions and stress-induced flowering with multivariate models in R.
2018 – 2020
Research Assistant — Ort Lab, UIUC Institute for Genomic Biology (RIPE project)
  • Managed transgenic tobacco and potato field trials, greenhouses, and growth chambers for the RIPE project under USDA-APHIS requirements. Collected gas exchange measurements in field and controlled conditions.
  • Performed Gibson and Golden Gate cloning, RNA extraction, cDNA library preparation, and virus-induced gene silencing.
  • Established sample-preparation methods and measured carbon and nitrogen content with elemental analysis.
  • Supervised five undergraduate assistants, with training in molecular methods and greenhouse safety.
2016 – 2017
Undergraduate Research Assistant — Crofts Lab, UIUC Biophysics
Studied electron transport kinetics by purifying cytochrome bc1 and incorporating the complexes into synthetic nanodiscs.
