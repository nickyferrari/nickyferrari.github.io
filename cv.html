Research experience

2022 – 2026
Graduate Research Assistant — Vinyard Lab, LSU Biological Sciences
  • Biophysics & spectroscopy: built an Empirical Wavelet Transform pipeline (Python/MATLAB) to decompose flash-induced fluorescence transients and separate the parallel S-state cycles of PSII. Ran JTS-150 pump-probe spectroscopy, P700 absorption, and OJIP fast fluorescence transients.
  • Instrumentation: designed and built an Arduino/Teensy-controlled LED flash lamp (C++, custom PCB with MOSFET gate drivers) delivering sub-millisecond saturating pulses. Reprogrammed PSI MC-1000 multicultivators in JavaScript to execute Total-Variation-matched stochastic and periodic light environments across eight parallel cultures.
  • Hardware design: parametric CAD in Fusion 360 for optical mounts, cuvette holders, and detector housings, fabricated by FDM 3D printing.
  • Algal physiology: maintained and characterized C. reinhardtii mutants under dynamic light in programmable growth chambers, optimizing acclimation protocols for fluorescence phenotyping.
  • Bioinformatics: screened 3,618 photosynthetic bacterial genomes for PSII assembly factors via a PostgreSQL + Python/Biopython TBLASTN pipeline on an HPC cluster, with a weighted scoring metric combining sequence similarity and predicted structural features.
  • Machine learning: developed an image-analysis pipeline to segment and quantify starch granules in microscopy images.
2020 – 2022
Graduate Research Assistant — South Lab, LSU Biological Sciences
  • Characterized photosynthetic performance of Arabidopsis thaliana photorespiratory mutants by PAM fluorometry (FluorCam 800-C) and simultaneous gas exchange (LI-COR 6800) under drought, heat, and fluctuating light. Completed LI-COR's week-long course on gas exchange theory and instrument energy-balance models.
  • Quantified transcriptional regulation of photorespiratory bypass genes under abiotic stress by qPCR and cDNA library construction.
  • Modeled genotype × environment interactions and stress-induced flowering acceleration using multivariate statistics in R.

Research Assistant — Ort Lab, UIUC Institute for Genomic Biology (RIPE project)
  • Managed transgenic Nicotiana tabacum and Solanum tuberosum field plots, greenhouses, and growth chambers under USDA-APHIS compliance. Ran field-scale and controlled-environment gas exchange measurements.
  • Performed high-throughput Gibson Assembly and Golden Gate cloning, RNA extraction, and cDNA library construction. Conducted virus-induced gene silencing in N. benthamiana.
  • Ran carbon/nitrogen elemental analysis (Velp CN-802) and established sample-preparation protocols.
  • Trained and supervised five undergraduate assistants in molecular protocols and greenhouse safety.
