Private backup utility

DEPI Machine Backups

A scheduled, one-way backup tool operated by Nicholas Ferrari to preserve selected DEPI project files in his Google Drive and make them available on his other computers.

Purpose

The utility copies selected presentations and digital-twin model resources, source code, documentation, generated outputs, and verification records from a local computer to dated Google Drive folders. It checks uploaded files against the local snapshot using checksums.

It uses rclone and a fixed backup script. It does not operate laboratory instruments, offer a public file-upload service, or provide public access to the backed-up files.

Google access

The owner authorizes Google Drive access through Google OAuth. The requested permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, limited to files and folders created by or explicitly made available to this application. The utility does not request access to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or all existing Drive files.

Backups are stored in the owner’s Google Drive. The utility does not enable public sharing or delete older snapshots automatically. Local working originals remain in place.

Privacy & support
