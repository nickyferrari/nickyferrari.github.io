Teaching

Photosynthesis is a good subject to teach because the machinery is invisible and the evidence isn't. My aim in a classroom is to get students to the moment where a measurement forces a conclusion — where a fluorescence trace or an oxygen yield says something that can't be argued with, and the model has to bend.

Mentoring

I have mentored undergraduate researchers across the Vinyard, South, and Ort labs, training them in qPCR, gas exchange, molecular cloning, and database scripting, and supervised five undergraduate assistants in molecular protocols and greenhouse safety during the RIPE project.

Since 2025 I have also served as a research mentor with the Illinois Science & Technology Coalition, advising high school students on experimental design and scientific writing.

Outreach & service

I judge and moderate at LSU Discover Day most years, and have judged plant sciences at the Louisiana Regional Science & Engineering Fair. I have talked about carbon fixation engineering and climate change as a guest interviewee for Harvard Extension School, and about agricultural photosynthesis research on WEFT 90.1 FM. I served as the student representative on a biochemistry faculty search committee in 2023.
